# Assumptions — M5 Import & Extraction

1. **Route permissions (revised by the independent review).** `/import/history` and the sheet wizards require `import.leads` (leads) or `import.properties` (property kinds), so executives get NoAccess there. `/import` requires only `import.document`: admins and managers see the full hub, and executives see a limited hub (document extraction, document samples, their drafts and their own document jobs). Master prompt §4 says executive bulk import is "❌ (or limited)", and ROLE_RESEARCH allows single-document extraction. This keeps the sidebar link off NoAccess (the smoke test requires that) without editing `app/nav.ts`. `/import/document`, `/import/drafts` and `/import/drafts/:id` stay on `import.document`.
2. **History visibility.** Admins see every job; managers see their own jobs plus every leads import. **Drafts:** executives see their own; anyone with `import.leads` sees all (matches the ownership rules in `services/drafts.ts`).
3. **Template headings and generated titles are English data.** The downloaded templates, their example rows and auto-generated listing titles ("3 BHK Apartment, Hebbal") are data exchanged as files, like names and notes, so they are not translated. Everything on screen goes through i18n (en + ta).
4. **Unmapped columns are never dropped.** For properties they are appended to the description as "Heading: value"; for leads to the lead's note (a `lead.noted` activity). `Property.specs` is not used for arbitrary keys, because other modules translate spec keys from the catalogue.
5. **City from locality.** A property row with a known locality but no city gets its city from the catalogue (confidence 0.85, no warning). The city column is then not reported as missing at the mapping step.
6. **Bare numbers under 1,000 are lakhs** for prices and budgets ("85" → ₹85 L), as in `lib/amount.parseAmountText`. Per-sq-ft rates ("9,800 / sqft") are multiplied by the row's area (super built-up first) and flagged.
7. **Row states.** A property missing a required fact (price, area, locality, BHK for homes, city) becomes a **Draft**. An unreadable value ("Call", a 5-digit phone, a bad e-mail) is **Needs fixing**. A lead missing its source or any contact is **Needs fixing**; a lead with a phone but no name becomes a Draft. Rows repeated inside the file are always skipped. Rows matching the CRM are skipped or update the existing record (the choice is on the Check step).
8. **"Update existing"** fills only the values present in the sheet. A lead's requirement is merged, never emptied. `lead.edit` is checked per lead.
9. **Lead import ownership.** Imported leads are created with `ownerId: null`, so E2 auto-assignment decides, or they stay unassigned when auto-assign is off. Assignment notifications are batched by the existing effect.
10. **Documents give one record per document**, except brochures with a unit table ("2 BHK · 1,265 sq ft · ₹98 L"): those give one property per unit row, sharing the rest of the text. A small rule pass (`lib/document.refineProperty`) uses the import's own readers on the unit row, the project (first line) and "ready by <month year>". The AI extraction (`extractLead` / `extractProperty`, owned by M7) does the rest.
11. **Lead source on documents** is left empty, because a visiting card does not say how the lead came in. It is asked for as a missing essential, or the record is kept as a Draft.
12. **The OCR engine and language data** load from tesseract.js's CDN on first use and are cached by the browser afterwards. Offline, the reader shows an honest error with "Paste text instead" and "Fill the form by hand". Scanned PDFs (no text layer) are rendered page by page (first 3 pages) and OCR'd.
13. **Brochure properties go live** when saved straight from a document (the user reviewed them). Sheet imports have a "Publish ready properties" switch (default on).
14. **Mapping profiles** are stored in `settings.mappingProfiles` through new functions in `services/imports.ts` (`saveMappingProfile`, `deleteMappingProfile`), with permission `import.leads` or `import.properties` by kind. The same name and kind replaces the earlier profile. The best-fitting profile (≥ 50 % of the headings) is applied automatically on upload.
15. **Removing a job from the history** (`removeImportJob`) keeps the records it created and can be undone for 10 s.
16. **Max file size is 10 MB**, the shared uploader's default. No row limit is enforced; the Check list paginates (25 per page).
17. The Tamil strings are drafted and need review by a fluent speaker.
