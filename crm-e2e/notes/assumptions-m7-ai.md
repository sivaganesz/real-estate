# Assumptions — M7 AI Assistants

1. **Hinglish** is handled by a rewrite step (`ai/simulated/hinglish.ts`) before the English readers: verb-final shapes ("X ko de do", "X ko negotiation mein daalo", "… fix karo", "… dikhao", "yaad dilana") and word swaps (kal, parso, aaj, baje, subah/shaam, tak, chahiye, mein, ka/ki/ke, mera naam). Pasted enquiries get only the word swaps (`hinglishWords`) so a message is never reshaped. The panel shows "Read as: …" whenever it rewrote a request. "kal" is read as tomorrow (the only useful sense for commands).
2. **Role chips** follow ROLE_RESEARCH §5, filled with real names from the data (a lead with an open follow-up, the executive on leave, the deactivated executive, the next visit's time). "Add lead from this WhatsApp message" prefills "Add lead: " so the user pastes the message. Admin "Deactivate Kavya…" became "Redistribute {deactivated exec}'s leads" because Kavya is already deactivated in the seed; deactivation itself is sent to Team (the assistant never deactivates users).
3. **Selected record** = the page's `useAISelection` if it sets one, else the record in the URL (`/leads/:id`, `/properties/:id`, `/appointments/:id`, `/clients/:id`, `/team/:id`, `/import/drafts/:id`, `?lead=` / `?property=` on Matching). A bare command ("budget now 1.5 Cr", "schedule a visit tomorrow 11am") applies to that record.
4. **Undo for every applied AI change**: `executeProposal` remembers the database just before running (one level, 10 s, `services/core/undo`), unless the service made its own snapshot. The toast and the card both offer Undo for 10 s (longer than the 6 s design default so card and toast agree).
5. **Assignments are permission-checked twice** (role in `executeProposal`, record in the service). Managers pick only their own team in the edit form; "auto" previews who would get each lead (pure `previewAssignments`) before Confirm.
6. **Stage moves that need a dialog** (Booked / Converted, Lost without a reason) are refused by `executeProposal` with a validation message (M6 R1). The reader proposes Lost only with a reason (read from the text, else "Other", editable on the card) and answers Booked with "use Mark as booked on the lead".
7. **"Within budget" / "under X"** filters matches by price ≤ the cap (the lead's budget max for "within budget").
8. **Overloaded** = more open leads than `settings.assignment.maxActivePerExec` when set, else more than 1.25 × the team average, or 3+ overdue follow-ups.
9. **Effort saved** is counted from records, never invented: AI activities (via 'ai') by the user today, AI-marked fields on records they created or updated today, auto assignments in scope today, automation tasks for them today. "About N min saved" uses fixed estimates (10 s per field, 45 s per assistant change, 60 s per assignment, 30 s per task) and says "about".
10. **Module assistants without a form** (the panel's Assistants tab) propose the record as a card: lead (`Add lead: …` through `parseCommand`), appointment (`Schedule appointment: …`), property (only when the listing has every essential; otherwise "Open the full form"). Inside a form (`onFill` given) they are the existing paste-and-fill box.
11. **Property creation from the assistant** sets `listing: 'live'`, `status: 'available'`, `transaction: 'resale'` unless the text says new booking, `possession: ready` unless stated, and empty media / approvals / specs.
12. **Drafted messages** stay in the template's language (English or Hinglish), never Tamil (client-facing data). Visit messages without a visit fall back to a plain follow-up instead of "your visit to at .". Opening WhatsApp from a draft logs the message on the lead (the click is the confirmation; K5).
13. **Follow-up timing**: new lead → within 15 min (working hours 9–20), hot → +2 h, warm → next working morning 11:00, cold → +3 days 11:00, just after a visit → same evening 18:00; Sundays move to Monday.
14. **Conversation** lives in a per-tab store (survives closing the panel and navigation, not persisted). The `aiPanelOpen` session flag is unchanged.
15. **"/"** opens the assistant and focuses its box when the user is not typing in a field (DESIGN_SYSTEM §14 rule 7 says "/" focuses search; the topbar search is "Search or ask AI").
16. **Live provider** gained `readUpdate`; it falls back per method like the others. README already documents the env vars and the backend-proxy rule, so no README change was needed.
17. **Tamil** strings in `ai` are drafted and need a fluent review; command text inside chips stays English (the simulated reader's language) while the chip labels are Tamil.
18. **Task titles the assistant writes** ("Call Priya Sharma") are generated in the UI language at the time they are proposed and then stored as data (they are editable on the card before Confirm).
