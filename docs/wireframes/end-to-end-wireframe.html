What happens behind the scenes

1. Search properties: matches the customer's words against each property's area, city and pincode, plus type, purpose and budget. Published properties only. 2. Property details: price, size, location, details, amenities. 3. Free slots: from Settings → Visit slots, blocked dates and existing bookings. 4. Book site visit: creates AP-000231 as Requested, linked to the conversation. The search may also save the customer's requirement.
Rules the AI always follows: answers only from our data; if something is missing it says so and offers a call back (never guesses prices, sizes, approvals or availability); never shares unpublished properties, internal fields, other customers' data or staff phone numbers; never promises discounts or legal outcomes; reads back property, date and time and waits for a clear “yes” before booking; politely declines anything outside these jobs and offers to pass it to the team.
Afterwards in the admin portal: the visit appears in Appointments (Requested), the chat in Conversations and the customer in Leads.

Closing status by purpose: Sale → Sold, Rent → Rented, Lease → Leased. Only Published properties are visible to the AI agent and on the website.

Appointment statuses

Requested→ Confirm →Confirmed→Completed·No-show·Cancelled

Bookings made by staff start as Confirmed. Bookings from the AI web chat and the website start as Requested (the phone number was typed by the customer) until staff confirm them. Requested or Confirmed visits can be rescheduled or cancelled. A slot can never take more visits than its limit.
